After the poem "Strong men" by Sterling Brown - https://poets.org/poem/strong-men published in "The Book of American Negro Poetry" by James Weldon Johnson https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/11986

Sterling Brown in a way is the Elvis Presley of his day, the Rick Rubin of his day, and should be studied. 
The poem "Strong men" was introduced to me by the dj Joost van Bellen who played a part of his dj set at a club in the Netherlands on one of the radio stations around 2001. 
The dj set started with some ambient sounds and the poem being read by an old voice, possibly Sterling Brown himself. 
Having recorded the dj set of the radio on a mini-disc, I've listened to it many times over the years. 
Generative AI is getting to a point where it doesn't need much input to produce something that can be appreciated, and so this poem was produced by one of Openai GPT models in early 2025. 
I'm still working on finding a good narration of it, because this will a much better way to experience it. Thoughts, suggestions very welcome. 
I don't have any message or meaning with the contents of the poem, other than appreciating its artistic value.
